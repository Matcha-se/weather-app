Three tabs in Android order: Home, Map, Places. Active tab is sky blue; inactive tabs are gray.

Keep it flat. No gradients, no 3D, no glow, no dark mode. Shadows stay under 8% opacity, and sky blue is the only accent. Every icon is an image file under src/assets/icons/ — replace one with your own PNG or SVG, keeping the same file name, and it shows up here and in the app.
